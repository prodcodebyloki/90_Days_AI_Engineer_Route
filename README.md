# 90 Days of AI Engineering

A Product Manager's journey to becoming an AI Engineer — one day at a time.

## The Challenge

I'm a Product Manager taking on a 90-day challenge to learn the skills of an AI Engineer from scratch. Every day I learn, build, and document the journey. Every day gets posted on YouTube.

## What to Expect

- Daily hands-on projects and experiments
- Real learning — including the failures
- Code, concepts, and tools covered day by day
- A PM's perspective on AI engineering

## Follow Along

Subscribe on YouTube to catch every day of the journey as it happens.

## Structure

Each day lives in its own folder (e.g. `Day2/`) with the code and notes from that session.

## Who Is This For

- Product Managers curious about AI Engineering
- Anyone wanting to learn AI/ML from a non-traditional background
- Builders who want to follow a real, unfiltered learning journey
